# Analytics and Fraud Rules

Analytics include total revenue, transaction count, average ticket size, month-over-month revenue growth, active/dormant merchants, subscriber totals and subscriber growth. Revenue series support daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly buckets.

The rule engine highlights revenue/subscriber movement above +30% or below -20%, dormancy after 90 days, failed-transaction ratios, and hourly velocity of at least 30 transactions. Risk scores are indicators for analyst review only; they are not proof of fraud. Tune thresholds using approved historical data and record rule versions before operational use.
